## Incubation Centre at VU

The VU Centre for Incubation and Entrepreneurship provides an opportunity for the students of BMS programs to convert innovative ideas into viable business plans and useful enterprises. The mentoring and other support systems which will be made Available at the Incubation Centre, will assist the students to translate their imagination into reality while learning theoretical concepts and conceptual models. Interested students will be able to earn sufficient credits to graduate in BMS with specialization in Entrepreneurship.
