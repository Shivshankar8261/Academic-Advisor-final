The School of Design at Vidyashilp University fosters global academic engagement through collaborations with leading international universities and design schools. Through exchange programs, summer schools, workshops, and visiting mentors, these collaborations immerse students in global design practices, diverse creative cultures, and cross-cultural learning experiences.

## F+F Zurich Exchange

The collaboration with the F+F School of Art and Design, Zurich, Switzerland, offers students opportunities to engage with contemporary European art and design education. Through exchange programs, workshops, and collaborative learning experiences, students participate in immersive studio environments that encourage experimentation, cultural exchange, and critical engagement with international creative practices.

The program enables students to develop global perspectives while experiencing different approaches to Design, art, and visual culture.

## Closing the Loop

Closing the Loop is an international social design Summer School organized by the Social Design Studio at the University of Applied Arts Vienna, Austria in collaboration with Vidyashilp University Centre for Art, Design and Innovation (VUCADI).

The program focuses on understanding the material and immaterial resources that shape contemporary cities and communities. Students engage with themes such as sustainability, urban culture, social innovation, ecological thinking, and community participation.

Through workshops, field engagement, lectures, and collaborative projects, participants work alongside internationally recognized artists, designers, researchers, and practitioners exploring the intersections of design, society, and environmental responsibility.
