## Higher Education Opportunities

The BDes program provides a strong foundation for advanced study in design, art, communication, media, technology, humanities, and interdisciplinary research. Graduates may pursue postgraduate education in areas such as:

* Communication Design
* Interaction Design
* Design Research
* Animation and Moving Image
* Film Studies
* Visual Culture
* Human-Computer Interaction
* Service and Systems Design
* Strategic Design
* Innovation Studies
* Digital Media
* Creative Technology
* Fine Arts and Contemporary Art Practices

The interdisciplinary nature of the curriculum equips students with the intellectual and professional foundation required for both specialised and transdisciplinary postgraduate studies.
