## Curriculum Structure

The Bachelor of Design (BDes) program is structured as a progressive four-year learning journey that combines foundational knowledge, creative exploration, disciplinary depth, professional practice, and independent inquiry.

The curriculum is designed to help students gradually develop visual, conceptual, technical, research, and professional competencies while building the ability to engage with complex social, cultural, technological, and ecological contexts.

Through a combination of design studios, workshops, seminars, field engagement, interdisciplinary electives, internships, and research projects, students learn to integrate creative thinking with critical inquiry and practical application.

The program follows a carefully sequenced structure that allows students to explore multiple design domains before pursuing focused specialization pathways.
