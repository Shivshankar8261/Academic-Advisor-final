## Career Pathways

The Bachelor of Design program prepares students for a wide range of careers across contemporary creative industries, emerging technologies, research, entrepreneurship, and social innovation. The interdisciplinary nature of the curriculum enables graduates to work across multiple domains while adapting to rapidly changing professional environments.

Students develop strong capabilities in communication, research, visualization, storytelling, systems thinking, problem-solving, collaboration, and creative leadership, allowing them to contribute meaningfully across diverse sectors.

Graduates may pursue careers in design studios, media organizations, technology companies, creative agencies, cultural institutions, research organizations, public systems, start-ups, and independent practice.
