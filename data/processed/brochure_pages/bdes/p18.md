## Emerging Professional Opportunities

As technology, media, and society continue to evolve, the role of designers is expanding beyond traditional professional boundaries. Contemporary designers increasingly shape products, services, systems, experiences, policies, and social interventions.

The program prepares students to engage with emerging fields, including:

* User Experience (UX) Design
* User Interface (UI) Design
* Interaction Design
* Service Design
* Design Research
* Brand Strategy and Communication
* Information Design
* Motion Graphics and Animation
* Illustration and Visual Narratives
* Editorial and Publication Design
* Experience Design
* Game Design
* Immersive Media (AR/VR/XR)
* Creative Technology
* Social Innovation and Public Interest Design
* Design Entrepreneurship

The curriculum emphasizes adaptability and lifelong learning, enabling graduates to remain relevant within evolving technological and professional landscapes.
