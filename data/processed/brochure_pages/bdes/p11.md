## Industry Engagement and Creative Dialogues

At Vidyashilp University, learning extends beyond the classroom through interactions with designers, artists, filmmakers, researchers, entrepreneurs, and cultural practitioners from diverse professional backgrounds.

These engagements expose students to contemporary creative practices, emerging ideas, and multiple perspectives shaping the future of design.

Workshops, masterclasses, artist talks, screenings, exhibitions, and collaborative projects offer students opportunities to engage directly with practitioners whose work spans communication design, visual culture, social innovation, technology, media, and contemporary art.

These experiences encourage students to connect academic learning with professional practice while developing curiosity, critical thinking, and creative confidence.

## Meditations on Paper

Led by Bengaluru-based graphic designer, illustrator, and educator Nupur Banerjee, this workshop explored drawing, illustration, storytelling, and visual expression as mindful and reflective creative practices.

Students engaged with observation, personal narratives, and experimental mark-making while discovering how creative processes can become tools for reflection, imagination, and self-expression. The workshop highlighted the relationship between artistic practice, attention, and meaningful visual communication.

## Gängeviertel: Self-Organized Art and Community Hamburg

Conducted by Hamburg-based artist and graphic designer Rita Kohel, this session introduced students to Gängeviertel, one of Europe's most recognized artist-led community initiatives.

Through discussions on creative resistance, collaborative living, cultural activism, and alternative urban spaces, students gained insights into how art and design can contribute to social engagement, collective action, and community-led cultural transformation.
