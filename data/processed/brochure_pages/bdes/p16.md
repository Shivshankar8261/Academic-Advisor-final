## Shilpites Showcase

The School of Design encourages students to present their work through exhibitions, public showcases, installations, screenings, and interdisciplinary events that celebrate creativity, experimentation, and innovation.

These platforms allow students to share ideas, receive feedback, engage with broader audiences, and develop professional confidence while showcasing the diversity of work emerging from the program.

The showcase culture reflects the school's commitment to creative exploration, public engagement, and collaborative learning.

## Design Show 2025

Design Show 2025 celebrated the creativity, imagination, and innovation of students across the School of Design.

The exhibition featured installations, visual communication projects, AI-inspired concepts, branding initiatives, social-impact projects, interactive experiences, and interdisciplinary explorations developed through studio learning and independent inquiry.

The event also hosted conversations with leading international practitioners, including Dr Jean-Marc Dépierre, Director of Alliance Française de Bangalore, and internationally recognized installation artist Tara Kelton, creating opportunities for dialogue between students, educators, and creative professionals.
