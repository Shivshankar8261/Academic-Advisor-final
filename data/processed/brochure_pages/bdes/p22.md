## Message from the Dean

We live in a time of profound transformation. Artificial intelligence, digital technologies, ecological challenges, and rapidly changing social realities are reshaping how we communicate, create, and work. In such a context, design cannot be understood merely as the creation of products, images, or services. It has become a critical way of understanding complexity, solving problems, and shaping meaningful futures.

At Vidyashilp University, we believe that design education must move beyond conventional models focused solely on technical skills. While professional competence remains essential, future designers must also possess critical thinking, conceptual clarity, adaptability, and an understanding of the socio-cultural, technological, and environmental contexts within which design operates.

The Bachelor of Design program at VU is conceived as an interdisciplinary and future-oriented learning environment that combines strong foundations in visual communication, interaction design, film, animation, digital media, storytelling, game design and emerging technologies with research, systems thinking, and creative inquiry. Through studio-based learning, industry projects, internships, workshops, field engagement, and independent exploration, students develop both professional expertise and intellectual depth.

Our goal is to prepare graduates for diverse opportunities in industry, entrepreneurship, research, and the creative economy. As technology increasingly automates production and execution, the designer's value lies in imagination, critical judgment, collaboration, and the ability to navigate complexity. We encourage students not only to learn how to design, but also to ask why, for whom, and with what impact.

At VU, we aspire to nurture thoughtful, skilled, and entrepreneurial designers who can contribute meaningfully to industry and society while shaping more sustainable, inclusive, and responsible futures.

Narendra Raghunath
Dean
School of Design
