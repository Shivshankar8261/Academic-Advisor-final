## Research, Innovation and Entrepreneurship

Design today plays an important role in innovation, knowledge creation, and social transformation. Students are encouraged to move beyond conventional problem-solving approaches and engage with research, experimentation, and entrepreneurial thinking.

Through independent projects, interdisciplinary collaborations, industry engagement, and research-led inquiry, students learn how to identify opportunities, develop ideas, test concepts, and create meaningful interventions.

The program supports students who aspire to establish creative practices, launch start-ups, pursue independent artistic careers, or contribute to research and innovation ecosystems.

## Industry Readiness

Professional preparation is embedded throughout the four-year learning journey. Students engage with real-world projects, internships, workshops, industry interactions, exhibitions, and collaborative initiatives that expose them to contemporary professional practices.
Rather than preparing students for a single profession, the program develops adaptable individuals capable of navigating multiple career pathways throughout their lives.

Emphasis is placed on portfolio development, communication skills, teamwork, critical thinking, project management, ethical decision-making, and professional responsibility.
These capabilities enable graduates to contribute effectively within diverse organisational, entrepreneurial, and independent contexts.
