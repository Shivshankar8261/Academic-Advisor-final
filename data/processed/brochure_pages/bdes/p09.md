## Film and Animation

This pathway combines narrative development, visual storytelling, motion design, animation, filmmaking, and emerging moving-image practices. Students explore both traditional and digital techniques while developing skills in storytelling, production, direction, editing, and visual communication.

Career opportunities include animation, filmmaking, motion graphics, content creation, digital media production, and visual storytelling.

## Comics and Sequential Art

This specialization focuses on narrative illustration, graphic storytelling, visual narratives, character development, world-building, and sequential image-making. Students explore the relationship between text, image, culture, and storytelling across contemporary media platforms.

Graduates may pursue careers in illustration, publishing, graphic novels, visual storytelling, editorial practice, and independent creative production.

## Game Design and Immersive Media (AR/VR/XR)

This pathway introduces students to interactive storytelling, game systems, immersive environments, extended reality technologies, virtual experiences, and emerging digital platforms.

Students learn to integrate narrative, technology, design thinking, and user engagement while developing innovative experiences for future media environments.

Potential career pathways include game design, immersive experience design, virtual production, XR design, interactive media, and emerging technology industries.
