Why Choose B.A. (Hons.) / (Hons. with Research) at VU?

Our Psychology program focuses on understanding the facets of human behavior and mental processes through foundational theories, interdisciplinary learning, research-based applications, critical thinking and ethical awareness.

VU offers BA (Hons with Research) for eligible students. Students opting for it, undertake a supervised research project in their final semester, which makes them eligible to apply for a PhD program after their BA degree.

Key Features

• Multidisciplinary foundations
• Rigorous Psychology major adhering to APA guidelines
• Regular updates to reflect latest research and trends
• Specialization tracks: Clinical, Counselling, and Cognitive Psychology
• Faculty experienced in both academia and industry
• Skill development for global citizenship
