## Lectures by Eminent Psychologists at VU
VU invites leading scholars and intellectuals from India and abroad to share their ideas on campus and elsewhere in Bengaluru. These events nourish the intellectual life at the university and inspire students in their pursuit of academic excellence.

Dr. Anup Dhar, Professor of Philosophy, BML Munjal University, delivered a talk, 'Psychoanalysis & Liberal Studies'.

Dr. Sabah Siddiqui, Assistant Professor of Psychology, SIAS, KREA University delivered a talk, 'The Unconscious Life of Ageing'.

Dr. Akila Kadambi, Postdoctoral Scholar at UCLA and USC delivered a talk, 'Neuroscience, Empathy, and Artificial Intelligence'.
