Gossip About Gotya – Psychological character exploration

A hilarious take on Erik Erikson's Psychosocial theory, The Chronicles of Gotya follows the life of Gotya - a dramatic, overconfident character - through every stage of Erikson's framework. Told through a gossip session, each life phase is exaggerated and entertaining, yet maps perfectly onto concepts like Trust vs Mistrust, Identity vs Role Confusion, and more.
