## MANODHARA
The Psychology Club, MANODHARA - derived from the Sanskrit words Mano (mind) and Dhara (flow) - embodies the dynamic stream of thoughts, emotions, and reflections that shape human experience. It offers a space beyond the classroom for students passionate about psychology to engage in experiential learning, dialogue, and mental health awareness initiatives.

The club's vision is to cultivate a vibrant community that fosters curiosity, empathy, and a deeper understanding of human behavior through research and action. MANODHARA's activities span academic and skill development, creative and cultural engagement, awareness and outreach, and research and career guidance.
