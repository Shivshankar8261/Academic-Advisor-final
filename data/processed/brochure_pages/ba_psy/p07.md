## Student Achievements & Faculty Collaborations

VU Psychology students have presented their research at reputed national conferences, addressing topics ranging from neurodiversity to virtual therapies, and showcased their academic excellence and social awareness.

## Select Conference Presentations

### Voices Unheard: Student Perspectives from Student Mental Health Advocates

Ms. Neha Rajesh Shelar, a third year BA (Hons.), Psychology student, under the guidance of Dr. Sheetal Lakhani and Dr. Gargi S Kumar, Assistant Professors of Psychology, presented her research at the 29th International and 60th National Conference of the Indian Academy of Applied Psychology, held in Nagaland in February, 2025.

### Improving Inclusion for Neurodivergent Adults in Learning Environments: Difficulties and Approaches

Ms. Khushboo Nayak, a third year BA (Hons.) Psychology student, presented her empirical research paper that focused on the systemic challenges faced by neurodivergent students in educational institutions, at the conference, Boundaries and Bridges in the Age of Disruption, FLAME University, Pune, March 19, 2025

### Photovoice Study on Coping Strategies in Body Image-Related Pressures Among Indian Students

Sivani Ajesh, a third year BA (Hons) Psychology student, co-presented a research paper at the 17th Young Psychologist Competition and Undergraduate Research Conference 2026 School of Psychological Sciences, CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Central Campus, Bengaluru, February 16, 2026. This paper, which won the third prize at the conference, explored how university students experience body image concerns and appearance-related pressures, and the coping strategies they use to navigate these challenges.

### Exploring the Psychological Effects of Virtual Reality Therapies in the Digital Age

Ms. Neha Rajesh Shelar presented her systematic literature review titled 'Exploring the Psychological Effects of Virtual Reality Therapies in the Digital Age', at the conference, Boundaries and Bridges in the Age of Disruption, FLAME University, Pune, March 19, 2025. It examined the clinical utility of VR-based interventions in treating psychological and physical conditions such as PTSD, anxiety, pain, and motor impairments.
