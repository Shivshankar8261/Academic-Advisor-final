The University Core curriculum builds critical thinking, analytical skills, and ethical awareness for responsible citizenship and interdisciplinary learning.

The Program Foundation courses lay a multidisciplinary foundation, integrating psychology with fields like gender studies and business management, while enhancing technical proficiency through tools like Python.

The Psychology Major combines rigorous theory and modern practices, preparing students for advanced studies and diverse professional roles.

Students can choose a Specialization Track - Clinical, Counselling, or Cognitive Psychology - to align with their career aspirations.

## Research and Internship
