Why Choose Law at VU?

The School of Law strives to deliver a transformative undergraduate education that equips law students with critical thinking, leadership qualities, and the necessary professional skills to address contemporary local and global challenges through a strong interdisciplinary lens.

## High Quality Education:
Students are trained to become ethical and skilled legal practitioners through academic rigor, experiential learning, and interdisciplinary exposure.

## Innovative Pedagogy:
A curriculum enriched with law clinics, moot courts, and experiential learning opportunities fosters intellectual and professional excellence.

## Commitment to Diversity:
The program emphasizes a deeper understanding of the law by appreciating diversity and fostering mutual respect.

## Global and Community Engagement:
Meaningful collaborations with communities, industries, and global academic networks prepare students for impactful careers in a rapidly evolving world.

## Strategic Location:
Situated in Bengaluru, the hub of innovation, VU provides exposure to the dynamic intersections of deep tech, commerce, art, finance, and business start-ups, with a focus on intellectual property and antitrust law.
