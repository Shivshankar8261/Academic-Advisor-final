Experiential Learning

## Session on LGBTQI + Community in India

In March 2026, 3rd-year students attended an engaging lecture by Prof. Shubha Chacko, Executive Director of Solidarity Foundation & gender-rights activist, on the LGBTQI+ community in India and the evolving legal frameworks. The session encouraged students to critically reflect on issues of equality, rights, and inclusion while exploring key legal developments shaping the discourse on gender and sexuality in India.

## Survey at Bettahalasooru Grama Panchayat

In March 2026, under the guidance of Karnataka State Legal Services Authority, students from the Legal Aid Committee, under the Legal Services Clinic initiative, completed a household survey covering 214 homes at Bettahalasooru Grama Panchayat.

## A visit to the Women’s Police Station in Yelahanka

On 20 November 2025, students visited the Women’s Police Station in Yelahanka as part of their course ‘Law of Crimes-II’, gaining first-hand exposure to the functioning of the criminal justice system and gender-sensitive policing.

## A visit to the SKF Factory, Bengaluru

Fifth-semester Law students, along with Law Minor students, visited the SKF factory in Bengaluru on 15 November 2025 as part of the practical component of Labour and Industrial Law - I, gaining real-world insights into industrial operations and strengthening their practical understanding of labour and industrial laws.
