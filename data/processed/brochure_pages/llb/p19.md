International Scholars at the School of Law

Martin van Hees, Distinguished Visiting Professor (Honorary) at VU, offered a two credit course titled, 'Moral Philosophy and Justice'. The course equips students to critically analyse moral responsibility, punishment, and competing perspectives on social and global justice.

## Legal Services Clinic

Vidyashilp University's vision of legal education extends beyond the classroom and has positioned itself as a catalyst for public service and equitable access to justice. As part of this commitment, the University has initiated a women-centric community-driven approach to legal literacy by engaging with Self-Help Groups, ASHA, and Anganwadi workers.

## Anganwadi workers' visit to VU

Students and Faculty actively participate in the Community Engagement Program, where they collaborate with local Anganwadis and government primary schools in the neighborhood to teach English, Mathematics, and Arts and Crafts, organize cleanup drives, and raise awareness about waste segregation.

## KSLA visit by students

VU's Legal Aid Clinic participated in a road safety legal aid workshop organized by the Karnataka State Police in collaboration with the Karnataka State Legal Services Authority (KSLSA), the Transport Department, Karnataka & Karnataka Traffic Police, as part of National Road Safety Month 2026.
