Program Structure of 5- year
Integrated Law Programs

Our B.A., LL.B. (Hons.) program is an
integrated law degree with a focus on
contemporary social and economic contexts,
while providing a wide-ranging exposure to
subjects in the humanities and social
sciences. The graduates from the program
are trained to critically look at the workings of
the law, its strengths and weaknesses, and
utilize its potential for improving society.

Our B.M.S., LL.B. (Hons.) program is an
integrated law degree program with a focus
on key managerial and business
administration subjects such as principles of
management,
financial markets, cost and management
accounting, organizational behavior,
financial management, mergers,
acquisitions and corporate restructuring,
technology laws, intellectual property laws,
international trade law, studied in
conjunction with the core law courses. This
course is designed to meet the emerging
needs of a fast-growing digital business
economy, preparing graduates to excel as
skilled legal professionals and managers.
