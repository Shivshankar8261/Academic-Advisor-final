Course Distribution Across the Program

The undergraduate journey of a law student at VU is thoughtfully structured to blend the University Core, Liberal Discipline major and minor courses, law core and clinical papers, and law honors courses, fostering a profoundly transformative legal education experience.

## Liberal Discipline - Major
Courses in ethics, critical thinking, and sustainability equip students to address real-world challenges with innovative and responsible solutions.

## Law Compulsory Clinical Papers
Practical training in advocacy skills, dispute resolution, legal drafting, and courtroom procedures to build essential professional expertise.

## Law Core Papers
A rigorous curriculum covering foundational, basic core subjects and advanced legal subjects to ensure a comprehensive understanding of the law.

## Law Honours Courses
Specialized coursework and research projects deepen legal knowledge, culminating in dissertation defence and viva voce.

## Additional Courses
Focused studies in legal history, research methodology, and statutory interpretation provide specialized tools for legal proficiency.

## Liberal Discipline Minor- Liberal Arts
Interdisciplinary courses in economics, psychology, business management, and criminology provide a broad perspective and essential skills for diverse professional paths.

## Language
Carefully crafted skill building courses in communication, academic writing, law and literature build articulation and analytical skills for legal and academic success.

- Law Core Papers: 33%
- Liberal Discipline - Major: 17%
- Law Compulsory Clinical Papers: 13%
- Additional Courses: 13%
- Liberal Discipline Minor- Liberal Arts: 10%
- Law Honours Courses: 9%
- Language: 5%
