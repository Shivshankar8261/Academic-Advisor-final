Workshops and Events

The School of Law hosts a range of workshops and events throughout the academic year, including the Law Special Lecture Series, featuring experts from diverse fields. These engagements broaden students’ perspectives, enrich their learning experience, and expose them to evolving legal discourse, contemporary debates, and varied schools of thought within the field of law.

## Raag Rang 2026

Students, faculty, and staff at Vidyashilp University brought the campus alive with Holi colour, music, and celebration during Rang-Raag 2026. Vibrant performances, games, festive stalls, and a soulful Carnatic music recital enthralled the audience.

## Draft IR (Karnataka) Rules, 2026

On 26 February 2026, the School of Law and the School of Business and Entrepreneurship at Vidyashilp University, in collaboration with the Centre for Labour Studies at NLSIU, hosted a roundtable on the Draft IR (Karnataka) Rules, 2026.

## Workshop on Road Safety

VU’s Legal Aid Clinic participated in a road safety legal aid workshop organized by the Karnataka State Police in collaboration with the Karnataka State Legal Services Authority (KSLSA), the Transport Department, Karnataka & Karnataka Traffic Police, as part of National Road Safety Month 2026.

## Workshop on Indian Constitutional History

To mark World Social Justice Day, the School of Law hosted a workshop on Indian Constitutional History, facilitated by Vineeth Krishna E, Senior Research Associate & Editor at the Centre for Law and Policy Research.

## VU Lecture Series by Dr. Neeraj Gill

Dr. Neeraj Gill explored the intersection of mental health and human rights, tracing the evolution of rights from classical ideas of justice to modern frameworks of dignity and autonomy.

## Constitution Day Celebrations, 26 November 2025

Students from the VU Core Course on Constitution and Human Rights showcased a thoughtful collection of posters that beautifully capture the principles of justice, equality, liberty, and democracy.
