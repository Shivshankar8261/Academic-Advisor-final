Why Choose the B.Tech. (Hons.) in Computer Science & Engineering (AI/ML) at Vidyashilp University?

Our B.Tech. (Hons.) in Computer Science & Engineering (AI/ML) Program is thoughtfully crafted to equip students with both the theoretical foundations as well as the practical skills needed to become successful professionals in this exciting and most impactful technology of the 21st century.

## Cutting-Edge Curriculum:
Integrates Computer Science and Engineering with advanced topics like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Natural Language Processing.

## Customizable Learning Pathways:
Students have the freedom to personalize their academic journey through a range of Elective Courses for a focused approach. Interdisciplinary minors in areas like Psychology, Design, Marketing, Finance, Law, and Economics further expand career possibilities, enabling students to operate at the intersection of data and domain knowledge. The open academic system fosters unique combinations of skills tailored to individual ambitions.

## Electives:
A key highlight of the program is the flexibility to choose from a diverse range of elective courses, such as Generative AI models, Parallel Computing, Probabilistic Graphical Models, Bayesian Methods in Machine Learning, Multi-Modal Learning, Data Visualization and Story Telling, Full Stack Development, Backend Systems and API Development, Big Data Analytics, Web Frameworks, Mobile Application Development, Performance Optimization & Testing, and Advanced Statistical Analysis.

## Interdisciplinary Opportunities:
