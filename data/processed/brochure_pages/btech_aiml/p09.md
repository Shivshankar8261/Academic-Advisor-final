Achievements

## Bengaluru Mobility Challenge

A collaborative team of students and faculty secured first place at IISc in the Bengaluru Mobility Challenge for developing AI-based objection, utilizing AI-based object detection models and VU's advanced GPU machines. By processing over 9 lakh images and identifying vehicles across 2000+ camera feeds, the team demonstrated cutting-edge innovation and outstanding teamwork.

## IEEE Leadership Summit 2024

Competing against 80 teams globally, our B.Tech. team clinched first place with Meera, a patient support robot known for its innovative design and functionality, reflecting the technical expertise and creativity nurtured at Vidyashilp University.

## Basha Techathon by Bhashini and Google Cloud

Our Data Science team received recognition for their AI-driven solutions to optimize the Indian Railways' complaint management system:

* Multi-modal AI Innovation was recognized as a Top Three Finisher.
* Advanced Voice Analysis AI was awarded with Special Jury's Recognition.

## AUTOWN'24 at BITS Pilani

Under the expert guidance of faculty, Dr. D.C.Kiran and Dr. N. Mehala, two teams from B.Tech 2nd year program, earned 1st and 3rd prizes respectively, for their outstanding technological solutions.
