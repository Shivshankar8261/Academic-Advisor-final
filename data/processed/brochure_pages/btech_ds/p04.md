# Course Distribution Across the Program

The undergraduate journey of a data science student at Vidyashilp University is meticulously crafted to integrate the University Core, Program Foundation, and Major discipline courses, fostering a transformative and enriching educational experience.

## University Core

The University Core curriculum equips students with critical thinking, problem-solving, and leadership skills, fostering responsible global citizens.

## Program Foundation

The Program Foundation courses establish a strong base in mathematics, computational thinking, and design, essential for advanced data science studies.

## Program Core

The Program Core focuses on practical and rigorous learning across various disciplines of computer science, including Artificial Intelligence and Internet of Things, shaping future-ready graduates.

## Program Honors

The Program Honors offers scholarly rigor in key areas such as Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Cloud Computing, equipping students with expertise in cutting-edge technologies.

## Specialization Track / Electives

A key highlight of the program is the Specialization Tracks, allowing students to focus on areas such as Applied Deep Learning or Full Stack Development to align with their career aspirations.

## Minor Discipline

Students can pursue Minors in diverse domains, fostering interdisciplinary knowledge and innovation.

## Research and Internship

Students also undertake Internships with leading companies gaining valuable industry exposure and practical experience.
