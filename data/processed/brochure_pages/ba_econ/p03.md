2 . VIDYASHILP UNIVERSITY

## Course Distribution Across the Program

The undergraduate journey of an economics student at VU is carefully designed to ensure that the synergy of University Core, Program Foundation, and Major Discipline courses creates a transformative learning experience.

* Majors
* Minors

## University Core

The University Core curriculum is designed to equip students with skills for effective problem-solving and responsible citizenship.

## Program Foundation

The Program Foundation courses establish a strong groundwork for a multidisciplinary approach to Economics by integrating essential courses from various fields.

## Economics Major

The Economics Major offers rigorous, practical, and quantitative courses that equip students to excel in professional roles and pursue advanced studies in their chosen fields.

## Minor Discipline

Students have the opportunity to pursue **Minors**, fostering interdisciplinary learning and preparing themselves to solve real-world challenges.

## Specialization Track

A key highlight of this program is the opportunity for students to choose a **Specialization Track** that aligns with their interests.

## Research and Internship

Students are required to undertake **Research Projects and Internships**, providing them with market exposure and practical job experience.
