## Why Choose B.A. (Hons.) / (Hons. with Research) at Vidyashilp University?

Our Economics program is thoughtfully curated to merge the principles of liberal arts with foundational and advanced economic theories, nurturing innovative thinkers and problem solvers.

VU offers BA (Hons with Research) for eligible students. Students opting for it undertake a supervised research project in their final semester, which makes them eligible to apply for a PhD program after their BA degree.
