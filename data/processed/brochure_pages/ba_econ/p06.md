The Four-year
Learning Journey at VU

## Year 1: Foundation
Build a strong foundation in the field of economics through courses in Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, and Mathematics.

## Year 2: Exploration
Explore global perspectives and quantitative techniques with econometrics, international economics, and game theory.

## Year 3: Specialization
Dive deep into financial economics, research methodologies, and advanced specializations in economics.

## Year 4: Proficiency
Master the art of economic inquiry through in-depth research, digital economics, and industry immersion.
