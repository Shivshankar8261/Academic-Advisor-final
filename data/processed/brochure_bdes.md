## B.Des. Communication Design: About the Program

The Bachelor of Design (BDes) program at Vidyashilp University is a contemporary design education program that prepares students to engage with the rapidly changing world of communication, technology, culture, and society.

As digital media, artificial intelligence, ecological concerns, and new forms of human interaction continue to reshape everyday life, designers are increasingly required to think beyond aesthetics and functionality to address complex human and societal challenges.

The program combines creative practice, critical inquiry, research, and technological understanding to help students develop as thoughtful and responsible designers. Through studio-based learning, interdisciplinary exploration, field engagement, and collaborative projects, students learn to create meaningful experiences, visual systems, narratives, products, and services that respond to contemporary realities.

Students build strong foundations in visual communication, interaction design, storytelling, animation, digital media, design research, and emerging technologies while developing the ability to think critically, work collaboratively, and communicate effectively across disciplines.

The curriculum encourages experimentation, innovation, and social responsibility while preparing students for careers across creative industries, communication design, digital media, entrepreneurship, research, and emerging design fields.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Why Choose BDes at VU?

Design today influences nearly every aspect of contemporary life. From digital platforms and communication systems to public services, social innovation, cultural production, and emerging technologies, Design plays a critical role in shaping how people interact with the world.

At Vidyashilp University, design education extends beyond technical skill development. Students are encouraged to explore the cultural, social, ecological, technological, and ethical dimensions of Design while developing practical skills relevant to professional practice.

The program offers a learning environment that combines academic rigor, creative experimentation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and industry engagement. Students work across diverse media and contexts while gaining exposure to contemporary design challenges through workshops, field projects, internships, research initiatives, and interactions with practitioners from around the world.

In an increasingly complex and interconnected world, the University's emphasis on critical thinking, creative adaptability, and responsible innovation enables students to develop both professional competence and intellectual confidence.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Learning Philosophy

The School of Design believes that meaningful learning emerges through making, questioning, observing, researching, collaborating, and reflecting. At Vidyashilp University's School of Design, we strongly believe that a design education should not merely train students to execute solutions, it should enable them to understand problems, challenge assumptions, and explore alternative possibilities.

The program adopts a studio-based, project-oriented approach, where students engage with real-world contexts while developing conceptual, creative, technical, and professional skills. Learning takes place through design studios, workshops, seminars, field engagement, independent projects, collaborative initiatives, and research-based inquiry.

Students are encouraged to cultivate curiosity, experimentation, empathy, and critical reflection. They learn to approach design not only as a professional discipline but also as a way of understanding society, culture, technology, and the environment.

By integrating theory and practice, the program nurtures designers who are capable of responding creatively and responsibly to contemporary challenges while remaining adaptable to future transformations.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Curriculum Structure

The Bachelor of Design (BDes) program is structured as a progressive four-year learning journey that combines foundational knowledge, creative exploration, disciplinary depth, professional practice, and independent inquiry.

The curriculum is designed to help students gradually develop visual, conceptual, technical, research, and professional competencies while building the ability to engage with complex social, cultural, technological, and ecological contexts.

Through a combination of design studios, workshops, seminars, field engagement, interdisciplinary electives, internships, and research projects, students learn to integrate creative thinking with critical inquiry and practical application.

The program follows a carefully sequenced structure that allows students to explore multiple design domains before pursuing focused specialization pathways.

<!-- page:7 -->
## B.Des. Communication Design: Year One: Foundation: Perception, Making and Creative Inquiry

The first year establishes the intellectual, creative, and technical foundations of design education. Students are introduced to the principles of visual communication, form, perception, material exploration, storytelling, drawing, design processes, and creative problem-solving.

The emphasis is on observation, experimentation, making, and critical thinking. Students develop an understanding of design as both a creative practice and a cultural process while building confidence in visual expression and communication.

Foundation studios are complemented by courses in design history, contemporary culture, communication studies, liberal arts, and emerging technologies, enabling students to develop broader perspectives on the role of design in society.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Year Two: Exploration: Systems, Media, and Research

The second year expands students' exposure to diverse design disciplines and emerging areas of practice. Through advanced studios and interdisciplinary projects, students engage with visual communication, interaction design, storytelling, digital media, animation, user experience, publication design, and design research.

Students begin to understand the relationships between technology, culture, communication, systems, and human behavior. Greater emphasis is placed on collaboration, research methods, contextual understanding, and project development.

This stage encourages students to identify their interests while developing the conceptual and technical skills necessary for advanced study.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Year Three: Specialization and Professional Development

The third year marks the transition from broad exploration to focused disciplinary engagement. Students choose a specialization pathway aligned with their interests, strengths, and career aspirations.

The curriculum deepens professional competencies through advanced studios, industry-linked projects, collaborative assignments, and research-based learning. Students engage with contemporary challenges while developing a distinctive creative voice and professional portfolio.

Workshops, visiting faculty interactions, industry collaborations, and internships provide opportunities to apply learning within real-world contexts and professional environments.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Year Four: Research, Innovation and Independent Practice

The final year focuses on independent inquiry, advanced project development, and professional preparation. Students undertake self-directed projects, design research, collaborative initiatives, and capstone work that reflect their interests and emerging professional identities.

The emphasis shifts from guided learning toward critical exploration, innovation, experimentation, and leadership. Students are encouraged to address complex contemporary issues through design while developing rigorous research, communication, and project-management skills.

The culmination of the program is a major graduation project that demonstrates creative excellence, intellectual depth, technical competence, and professional maturity.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Specialization Pathways

At the end of the fourth semester, students select a specialization pathway to develop deeper expertise in a chosen area of design practice.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Interaction Design

Interaction Design focuses on the design of digital products, services, interfaces, and user experiences. Students learn to create intuitive, meaningful, and engaging interactions across digital platforms while exploring emerging technologies, user behavior, systems thinking, and experience design.

Potential career pathways include UX/UI design, product design, service design, experience design, design research, and digital innovation.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Graphic Design

Graphic design explores visual communication across print, digital, environmental, and interactive media. Students engage with typography, branding, publication design, information design, visual systems, and communication strategy while developing strong conceptual and aesthetic capabilities.

Graduates may pursue careers in branding, editorial design, visual communication, creative direction, publication design, and communication strategy.

<!-- page:9 -->
## B.Des. Communication Design: Film and Animation

This pathway combines narrative development, visual storytelling, motion design, animation, filmmaking, and emerging moving-image practices. Students explore both traditional and digital techniques while developing skills in storytelling, production, direction, editing, and visual communication.

Career opportunities include animation, filmmaking, motion graphics, content creation, digital media production, and visual storytelling.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Comics and Sequential Art

This specialization focuses on narrative illustration, graphic storytelling, visual narratives, character development, world-building, and sequential image-making. Students explore the relationship between text, image, culture, and storytelling across contemporary media platforms.

Graduates may pursue careers in illustration, publishing, graphic novels, visual storytelling, editorial practice, and independent creative production.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Game Design and Immersive Media (AR/VR/XR)

This pathway introduces students to interactive storytelling, game systems, immersive environments, extended reality technologies, virtual experiences, and emerging digital platforms.

Students learn to integrate narrative, technology, design thinking, and user engagement while developing innovative experiences for future media environments.

Potential career pathways include game design, immersive experience design, virtual production, XR design, interactive media, and emerging technology industries.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Meditations on Paper

Led by Bengaluru-based graphic designer, illustrator, and educator Nupur Banerjee, this workshop explored drawing, illustration, storytelling, and visual expression as mindful and reflective creative practices.

Students engaged with observation, personal narratives, and experimental mark-making while discovering how creative processes can become tools for reflection, imagination, and self-expression. The workshop highlighted the relationship between artistic practice, attention, and meaningful visual communication.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Gängeviertel: Self-Organized Art and Community Hamburg

Conducted by Hamburg-based artist and graphic designer Rita Kohel, this session introduced students to Gängeviertel, one of Europe's most recognized artist-led community initiatives.

Through discussions on creative resistance, collaborative living, cultural activism, and alternative urban spaces, students gained insights into how art and design can contribute to social engagement, collective action, and community-led cultural transformation.

## B.Des. Communication Design: A Breath Held Long

Students experienced a special screening and discussion of A Breath Held Long, an evocative short film by acclaimed Mumbai-based artist Sudarshan Shetty.

The film explores themes of memory, displacement, belonging, urban life, and human relationships within Mumbai's emotional and cultural landscape. The screening offered a unique opportunity to engage with contemporary artistic practice through cinema, narrative, and visual storytelling.

The event encouraged reflection on the ways artists use moving images to explore personal, social, and cultural realities.

Collaborations

## B.Des. Communication Design: F+F Zurich Exchange

The collaboration with the F+F School of Art and Design, Zurich, Switzerland, offers students opportunities to engage with contemporary European art and design education. Through exchange programs, workshops, and collaborative learning experiences, students participate in immersive studio environments that encourage experimentation, cultural exchange, and critical engagement with international creative practices.

The program enables students to develop global perspectives while experiencing different approaches to Design, art, and visual culture.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Closing the Loop

Closing the Loop is an international social design Summer School organized by the Social Design Studio at the University of Applied Arts Vienna, Austria in collaboration with Vidyashilp University Centre for Art, Design and Innovation (VUCADI).

The program focuses on understanding the material and immaterial resources that shape contemporary cities and communities. Students engage with themes such as sustainability, urban culture, social innovation, ecological thinking, and community participation.

Through workshops, field engagement, lectures, and collaborative projects, participants work alongside internationally recognized artists, designers, researchers, and practitioners exploring the intersections of design, society, and environmental responsibility.

## B.Des. Communication Design: Message from the Dean

Message from Narendra Raghunath, Dean (B.Des. Communication Design).

We live in a time of profound transformation. Artificial intelligence, digital technologies, ecological challenges, and rapidly changing social realities are reshaping how we communicate, create, and work. In such a context, design cannot be understood merely as the creation of products, images, or services. It has become a critical way of understanding complexity, solving problems, and shaping meaningful futures.

At Vidyashilp University, we believe that design education must move beyond conventional models focused solely on technical skills. While professional competence remains essential, future designers must also possess critical thinking, conceptual clarity, adaptability, and an understanding of the socio-cultural, technological, and environmental contexts within which design operates.

The Bachelor of Design program at VU is conceived as an interdisciplinary and future-oriented learning environment that combines strong foundations in visual communication, interaction design, film, animation, digital media, storytelling, game design and emerging technologies with research, systems thinking, and creative inquiry. Through studio-based learning, industry projects, internships, workshops, field engagement, and independent exploration, students develop both professional expertise and intellectual depth.

Our goal is to prepare graduates for diverse opportunities in industry, entrepreneurship, research, and the creative economy. As technology increasingly automates production and execution, the designer's value lies in imagination, critical judgment, collaboration, and the ability to navigate complexity. We encourage students not only to learn how to design, but also to ask why, for whom, and with what impact.

At VU, we aspire to nurture thoughtful, skilled, and entrepreneurial designers who can contribute meaningfully to industry and society while shaping more sustainable, inclusive, and responsible futures.

Narendra Raghunath
Dean
School of Design
