## Infrastructure rules

- The campus has three blocks (A, B and C). Each block has a ground floor with a lobby, plus a first, second and third floor.
- Every classroom has exactly 2 CCTV cameras.
- Every classroom has 1 TV/display screen: a large interactive touch screen used for presenting and writing.
- Every classroom has student chairs, tables and a faculty desk.
- Each classroom floor has 6 classrooms.
- Air conditioning is primarily available in computer labs.
- The third floor of each block is designated exclusively for faculty offices and faculty-related spaces.
- Every floor has washrooms.
- CCTV cameras cover corridors and the whole campus, except washrooms/restrooms.

## Layout notes

- Floors: Ground Floor (lobby, library, seminar halls, meeting rooms, administrative spaces) and First, Second and Third floors.
- Room numbers follow BLOCK-FLOOR+SEQ: A-001 is on the ground floor, A-101 on the first floor, A-201 on the second, A-301 on the third.
- Classrooms are on the first and second floors of every block, 6 per floor.
- CCTV covers corridors, lobbies, entrances, pathways, parking and open areas; washrooms have no cameras.
- The display screen in every classroom is a large interactive touch screen used for display and writing.
