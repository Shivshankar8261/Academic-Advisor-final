## Ph.D. (PhD, doctoral) programmes and research areas

VU offers 8 Ph.D. (PhD) programmes. Duration and credits are not stated in the sheet.

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**: research areas Image Processing, Internet of Things, Algorithms
- **Ph.D. in Data Science**: research areas Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, NLP, Deep Learning, Multi-modal Learning, Audio & Video Analytics
- **Ph.D. in Mathematics & Statistics**: research areas Fractional Calculus, Wavelets, Statistics
- **Ph.D. in Material Science**: research areas Gas Sensors, Photocatalytic Dye Degradation, Water Splitting
- **Ph.D. in Economics**: research areas International Economics, Gender Economics, Indian Economics
- **Ph.D. in Psychology**: research areas Visual Perception, Attention, Memory
- **Ph.D. in Sociology & Anthropology**: research areas Urban Studies, Phenomenology of Religion, Marriage and Kinship, Migration and South Asian Diasporas
- **Ph.D. in Law**: research areas Human Rights Law, Sanitation Law and Policy, Environmental Law, Administrative Law, Constitutional Law, Technology Law and AI Ethics, Law and Gender Justice
