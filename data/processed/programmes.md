## B.Tech (Hons) Computer Science & Engineering - Data Science (BTech Data Science, B.Tech DS, CSE Data Science)

B.Tech (Hons) Computer Science & Engineering - Data Science (also written BTech Data Science, B.Tech DS, CSE Data Science) is a 4-year, 8-semester undergraduate programme of the School of Engineering and Technology. It requires 180 credits in total.

- Degree: B.Tech (Hons)
- Variants: Hons
- Major: Data Science
- Minor options: Economics; Psychology; Design; Marketing; Finance; Law

### B.Tech (Hons) Computer Science & Engineering - Data Science: eligibility and admission

Eligibility for B.Tech (Hons) Computer Science & Engineering - Data Science (BTech Data Science, B.Tech DS, CSE Data Science): 10+2, min 50% overall; Physics & Mathematics compulsory. Entrance / selection: KCET / JEE Main / COMEDK / CUET / state CETs.

### B.Tech (Hons) Computer Science & Engineering - Data Science: minors available

Students of B.Tech (Hons) Computer Science & Engineering - Data Science (BTech Data Science, B.Tech DS, CSE Data Science) can take a minor in: Economics, Marketing, Psychology, Finance, Design, Law.
