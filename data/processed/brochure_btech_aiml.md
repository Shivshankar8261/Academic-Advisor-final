## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: Learning Journey
Across Academic Years

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: Year 1: Foundation
Acquire a robust foundation in Mathematics and Computer Science by learning topics such as Calculus, Linear Algebra, Data Structures and Programming, analytical problem solving skills etc.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: Year 2: Exploration
Gain a good background in Computer Science by learning topics such as Computer Organization, Algorithms and advanced programming concepts. Get a good understanding of the field by learning basics of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, statistical methods, and Data Analysis techniques.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: Year 3: Specialization
Dive deeper into AI/ ML by studying topics such as Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, ML System Development, Cloud Computing etc.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: Year 4: Proficiency
Acquire technical competencies through advanced elective courses, capstone project and industry internships.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: Bengaluru Mobility Challenge

A collaborative team of students and faculty secured first place at IISc in the Bengaluru Mobility Challenge for developing AI-based objection, utilizing AI-based object detection models and VU's advanced GPU machines. By processing over 9 lakh images and identifying vehicles across 2000+ camera feeds, the team demonstrated cutting-edge innovation and outstanding teamwork.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: IEEE Leadership Summit 2024

Competing against 80 teams globally, our B.Tech. team clinched first place with Meera, a patient support robot known for its innovative design and functionality, reflecting the technical expertise and creativity nurtured at Vidyashilp University.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: Shilpites Showcase

AI/ML students at Vidyashilp University work on challenging projects that help them develop strong analytical skills and a deeper understanding of the domain. The projects focus on solving real-world problems through data-driven decisions.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: VURA

VURA combines the power of speech and image recognition, showcasing the innovative spirit of students at VU. By integrating AI, machine learning, and natural language processing, it highlights how students apply cutting-edge technologies to create interactive, real-world solutions.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: FORENSIC SKETCHING ROBOT

The AI-powered forensic sketching robot combines facial landmark detection, speech-to-text, and cloud computing to address the limitations of traditional forensic sketching. By providing an interactive, AI-enhanced system, it allows for more precise suspect identification, reducing human error and bias while ensuring greater forensic accuracy and credibility.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: FORENSIC VISION

Leveraging computer vision and UV light, Forensic Vision enhances crime scene evidence detection by reducing human error and bias. It offers a secure, transparent process for evidence logging and filing, ensuring tamper-proof records and the legal admissibility of forensic data while improving the accuracy of investigations.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: ULTRASONIC DISTANCE SENSOR

Redefining precision in measurement, the Ultrasonic Distance Sensor enhances applications in Autonomous Navigation, Healthcare Robotics, and Marine Research, ensuring accurate data for critical operations.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - AI & ML: AUTOMATED FIREFIGHTER

Designed for rapid response and pinpoint accuracy, the Automated Firefighter is set to revolutionize firefighting and search and rescue missions, improving search and rescue in high risk environments.
