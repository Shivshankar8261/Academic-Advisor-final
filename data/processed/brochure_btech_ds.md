## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: Learning Journey
Across Academic Years

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: Year 1: Foundation
Build a robust foundation in computer science and mathematics through courses like Data Structures, Calculus, and Linear Algebra. Develop essential analytical and problem-solving skills.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: Year 2: Exploration
Explore advanced computational concepts and data science techniques, including Programming in Python, Probabilistic Graph Models, and Statistics. Strengthen your understanding of theoretical and applied computer science.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: Year 3: Specialization
Dive deep into specialization tracks such as Applied Deep Learning or Full Stack Development, gaining expertise in advanced topics like Natural Language Processing, Big Data Analytics, and Cloud Computing.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: Year 4: Proficiency
Master technical and interdisciplinary competencies through a capstone project (1 semester), industry internships, and courses like Internet of Things and AI Model Deployment.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: Shilpites Showcase

Data Science students at Vidyashilp University work on challenging projects that help them develop strong analytical skills and a deeper understanding of the domain. Their projects focus on solving real-world problems through data-driven decisions.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: VURA

VURA combines the power of speech and image recognition, showcasing the innovative spirit of students at VU. By integrating AI, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing, it highlights how students apply cutting-edge technologies to create interactive, real-world solutions.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: FORENSIC SKETCHING ROBOT

The AI-powered forensic sketching robot combines facial landmark detection, speech-to-text, and cloud computing to address the limitations of traditional forensic sketching. By providing an interactive, AI-enhanced system, it allows for more precise suspect identification, reducing human error and bias while ensuring greater forensic accuracy and credibility.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: FORENSIC VISION

Leveraging computer vision and UV light, Forensic Vision enhances crime scene evidence detection by reducing human error and bias. It offers a secure, transparent process for evidence logging and filing, ensuring tamper-proof records and the legal admissibility of forensic data while improving the accuracy of investigations.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: ULTRASONIC DISTANCE SENSOR

Redefining precision in measurement, the Ultrasonic Distance Sensor enhances applications in Autonomous Navigation, Healthcare Robotics, and Marine Research, ensuring accurate data for critical operations.

## B.Tech. (Hons.) CSE - Data Science: AUTOMATED FIREFIGHTER

Designed for rapid response and pinpoint accuracy, the Automated Firefighter is set to revolutionize firefighting and search and rescue missions, improving search and rescue in high risk environments.
