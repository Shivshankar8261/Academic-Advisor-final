## B.M.S. (Hons.) Digital Business: Eco-Tility

Empowering sustainable living, the
ECO-TILITY app blends augmented
reality and object detection to help users
create eco-conscious spaces. Addressing
growing concerns about climate change,
this app provides tailored
recommendations for sustainable
materials and plant placements,
promoting environment-friendly choices
for homes and offices.

## B.M.S. (Hons.) Digital Business: Jholastic

Tackling global textile waste, JHOLASTIC
repurposes old jeans into fashionable,
eco-friendly bags. By addressing waste
management and promoting sustainable
fashion, JHOLASTIC offers a unique
solution for consumers looking to reduce
their environmental impact while staying
stylish, all through a circular fashion model.

## B.M.S. (Hons.) Digital Business: Nyaya Mitra

Focused on making legal services accessible,
Nyaya Mitra aims to provide affordable legal
solutions for low-income individuals and
small businesses. The platform offers
customizable legal templates, educational
resources, and case management tools, all
designed to democratize access to justice
and empower vulnerable communities.

## B.M.S. (Hons.) Digital Business: Planaro

Redefining event planning, Planaro is an AI-powered platform that simplifies coordination, budgeting, and guest management. With features like real-time guest tracking, virtual 3D event visualization, and sustainable event options, Planaro streamlines the planning process for both physical and virtual events, enhancing the overall experience for organizers and guests.

## B.M.S. (Hons.) Digital Business: SmartSaver

Addressing food waste in the restaurant industry, SmartSaver is an all-in-one platform that integrates inventory tracking, demand forecasting, and automated food donations. By reducing waste and redistributing surplus food to local charities, SmartSaver helps restaurants increase profitability while contributing to social good, all within one seamless platform.

## B.M.S. (Hons.) Digital Business: Message from the Dean

Vidyashilp University is a young educational initiative located in Bangalore and the School of Business and Entrepreneurship offers 4-year undergraduate programs in management under the guidelines of the new education policy. Honours Bachelor in Management Studies (BMS), Honours Bachelor in Management Studies (BMS with Research) and B.M.S., LL.B. (Hons) are options available for students. The school also offers doctoral program in management.

The School of Business and Entrepreneurship endeavors to create an innovative and collaborative learning environment hoping to nurture sensitive, responsive and empathetic business leaders and entrepreneurs for a rapidly changing socio-economic environment, both globally and locally. The learning pedagogy is experiential, case-based, supported by industry interns and stimulated by simulations. Exposure to diverse ideas and thoughts from the multi-disciplinary ecosystem of VU and the opportunity to ideate and create enterprises with assistance from the incubation centre will provide a unique experience of learning by practice.

Happy to welcome you to the School of Business and Entrepreneurship at Vidyashilp University.

Kuriakose Mamkoottam
Professor & Dean
